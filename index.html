Intensive English for Restaurant and Food Processing

A 12-week plan for beginners, built around the kitchen, the till and the production line.

Assessment & documentation

Ongoing

  • Every week: exit tickets, Kahoot/Wordwall checks, peer feedback rounds — plus a quick check of that week's grammar point.
  • Portfolio: a running digital folder (Google Docs/OneNote) with each week's written product, assessed against a simple shared criteria list.

Milestones

MID-TERM · WEEK 44

Oral role-play + retelling in past simple — vocabulary, pronunciation, structure, grammar accuracy.

END-OF-TERM · WEEK 50

Teacher-led oral talk — the student introduces themselves, explains their vocational line and why they chose it, then answers teacher questions covering the topics from week 38–50, using present, past and future confidently.
